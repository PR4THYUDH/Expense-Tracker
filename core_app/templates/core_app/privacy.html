Privacy & Data Handling Policy

Effective date: 2026. Explicit breakdown of data storage, user isolation, and Claude AI API processing.

1. Data Storage & Multi-Tenant Isolation

All expense records, budget allocations, and user profiles are stored in an encrypted relational database (PostgreSQL in production, SQLite in local development). Every database query is strictly parameterized and filtered by the authenticated user's ID (request.user).

Users cannot access, modify, delete, or export any other user's financial records through URL manipulation, form tampering, or direct query alteration.

2. How Anthropic Claude AI is Used

FinTrack AI employs the Anthropic Claude API for three distinct, opt-in assistance capabilities:

  • Receipt Scanning: When you upload a receipt photo, the image is transmitted directly to Claude's multimodal vision API over encrypted TLS connections solely to extract merchant, date, amounts, and items. The extracted fields are presented for your explicit review and editing before anything is saved.
  • Spending Insights: To generate advisory insights, our backend aggregates high-level monthly totals and category percentages. We never transmit bank account numbers, passwords, or personal identity numbers.
  • Natural Language Questions ("Ask AI"): When you submit a question, pre-calculated, verified statistics from your account are injected into the prompt. Claude is never given direct or raw SQL execution permissions to your database.

3. Data Retention & Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your financial information or transaction records to third parties or advertising brokers. Data sent to the Anthropic API is processed in accordance with Anthropic's Commercial Terms of Service and data privacy policies.

4. User Best Practices

We recommend that users redact sensitive personal identification numbers (e.g., PAN, Aadhaar, SSN) before uploading receipts or adding transaction notes.
